Update Log

Day 7

Cordelia gets its own day/night atmosphere and the camera now works consistently across every celestial body. Moon Quartz and the Upgraded Rocket Engine arrive, with 200% fuel capacity. Accounts, achievements, account-wide Gems, rebindable controls, and LMB keybinding are added. A new orange-striped hat stall brings character customization with colors and hats, plus the Credits page. Day 7 is officially complete!

Day 6

Cordelia becomes fully launchable with a dedicated takeoff and escape sequence. Ivis stays visible from deep space, spaceflight now has emergency fuel-loss recovery to prevent softlocks, and the scythe gets a rebuilt curved blade. Day 6 is officially complete!

Day 1

Pocket Universe begins: the small spherical planet, terrain generation, player movement, jumping, sprinting, the day/night cycle, and the first world visuals.

Day 2

Survival systems arrive: crystals, inventory and hotbar, crafting, tools and durability, trees and mining, the furnace, merchant, credits, and a growing set of world interactions.

Day 3

The project is reorganized into a proper multi-file project. Save/load, Survival and Freeplay, the rocket, launch pad, engine and fuel systems, and full spaceflight are added.

Day 4

The world gets atmosphere: weather, clouds, rain and thunderstorms, lightning, stars, space visuals, particles, ambient sounds, tool animations, the World Control menu, and more polish.

Day 5

The Moon is added as a real celestial body with its own collision and gravity. The rocket can land on the Moon, the player can explore it with 2× jumping and lunar dust, and the full Moon ↔ Ivis return loop works. Rocket speed modes add Boosted and WARP travel.
