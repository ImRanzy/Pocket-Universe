Update Log

LATEST

Version 1.4A

Day 14: survival systems, Beobaka, a new player model, character customization, clothing, animation polish, and screenshot-friendly UI controls.

  • Added Beobaka, Ivis's new alien bunny wildlife species, with hopping, fleeing, tree avoidance, surface-aware movement, and a maintained population of 10.
  • Added the Beobaka harvesting and meat system: axe harvesting, red-flash/fall reaction, smoke despawn effect, hidden replacement spawning, and Raw Beobaka drops.
  • Added the Campfire cooking system, including placing Raw Beobaka near a campfire, cooking it over time, and producing Cooked Beobaka as a world item.
  • Added hunger and health: 100 Hunger, 100 Health, hunger-based movement/stamina penalties, starvation damage, and item-based hunger restoration.
  • Added Raw Beobaka for +10 Hunger and Cooked Beobaka for +30 Hunger, with the cooked food also restoring stamina.
  • Added the new Player.glb character model, replacing the old third-person player body and integrating the existing hat and item-hand systems.
  • Added new player walking, sprinting, jumping, eating, and Minecraft-style crouch animations, including matching arm/leg motion and smooth crouch transitions.
  • Added held-item hand attachment so tools and food move with the player's hand during movement and animations.
  • Added the new shirt model and sleeve pivots, with the shirt following the torso and sleeves following the animated arms.
  • Added Account character customization with a 3D player replica and 12 selectable skin colors, saved to the account.
  • Linked Helna's purchased color to the player's equipped shirt color while keeping skin color separate.
  • Added a Screenshot UI toggle on the Backquote (` / ~) key to hide gameplay HUD elements without pausing the game.
  • Expanded key rebinding so Screenshot UI and Escape/pause bindings can be restored and rebound correctly.
  • Added further player presentation and UI polish, including improved miniature character previews and smoother character-animation integration.

Version 1.3A

Day 13: wildlife, living planets, environmental polish, and a larger star field.

  • Added 3D wildlife across the planetary system: Ivis birds, Ivis butterflies, Cordelia silverfish, Aurora glowfish, and Mileria rock crawlers.
  • Added bird flocks of up to five birds flying in V formations, with routes between all four Ivis mountains and bird-to-bird avoidance.
  • Added butterfly flower behavior, with 90 butterflies distributed around 20 flower patches and fleeing to other patches when approached.
  • Added Cordelia silverfish behavior: sparse, slow-moving insects that prefer the sunlit side and hide inside nearby cacti when approached.
  • Added Aurora Glowfish that swim in small schools around Aurora's lakes and scatter when the player gets close.
  • Added Mileria Rock Crawlers, rare crystal-like creatures that dart away when disturbed, with varied mineral hues and subtle glints.
  • Fixed ambient particle attachment so snow, falling leaves, and crystal particles follow their celestial body instead of being dragged away by planetary orbits.
  • Expanded the star field to surround the full solar system, centered on the Sun rather than forming a local sphere around Ivis.

Version 1.2A

Day 12: the Concierge, deliveries, exploration tools, weather, and major world-system polish.

  • Added the telephone booth on Ivis and the new Concierge contact, with keypad dialing and interactive dialogue.
  • Added the full delivery rocket system, including delayed delivery, final descent, order confirmation, pickup, and the optional 2× expedited delivery service.
  • Expanded the Catalogue and lore system with Concierge and Helna entries, Survival-only contact notes, and the Blueprint catalogue entry.
  • Integrated Helna's hat shop into Concierge dialogue without blocking the surrounding booth interface.
  • Added the Compass HUD and Sleeping Bag, including nighttime sleep, accelerated time progression, placement, pickup, and saved state.
  • Expanded the Space Map with planet-specific views, resource information, pan/zoom controls, and a toggleable Habitable Zone.
  • Adjusted Cordelia's orbit to a clean circular path and improved orbital/map alignment and planetary movement behavior.
  • Added and refined weather and lighting, including localized rain/cloud behavior, dynamic sunlight, night-side darkness, solar-glare fading, and local artificial lights.
  • Fixed rocket exit positioning on orbiting Ivis and improved delivery-rocket landing and departure behavior.

Version 1.1A

Day 11: polish, planetary motion, new crafting technology, storage, and major quality-of-life improvements.

  • Added polished holographic UI feedback, including smoother panels, button feedback, tooltips, and inventory slot animations.
  • Added Space Map pan and zoom, plus a redesigned Reset View button and improved map presentation.
  • Added planet-to-space transition effects and renamed the old random-plane state to Deep Space.
  • Added inventory hover tooltips with cursor-following positioning and automatic screen-edge handling.
  • Added inventory pickup popups for clear item collection feedback.
  • Added a permanent Ivis solar orbit, with the Moon continuing to orbit Ivis while Ivis orbits the Sun.
  • Added planetary rotation flight assistance, allowing rockets to naturally follow a world's rotation while inside its gravity region and stop carrying at Deep Space.
  • Extended landed-rocket and placeable-object attachment to Ivis, the Moon, Cordelia, Aurora, and Mileria, so rockets and placeables stay with their celestial body.
  • Added Iron Wrench and Titanium Wrench tools for installing different tiers of rocket upgrades.
  • Added Iron Plates and Titanium Plates with ingot-to-plate crafting.
  • Added the full Blueprint system: six permanent blueprints sold by Jaecob and required to unlock Rocket, Engine Mark 1–3, and Warp Drive Mark 1–2 recipes.
  • Added the Container: a craftable 20-slot storage block with separate inventories per container, using the new metallic storage UI.
  • Added and refined Titanium processing, including Titanium smelting, tougher Titanium deposits, and dedicated Titanium mining/collection feedback.
  • Added dedicated Rainbow Opal collection audio and Titanium deposit mining audio using the standard crystal-pickup and mining sound sets.
  • Smoothed Mileria's terrain and evenly distributed Titanium deposits across its surface.
  • Improved Aurora lakes and expanded its lush environmental presentation.
  • Improved the Space Navigation Map orbit presentation so Syspo's displayed orbit aligns with its actual planetary path.
  • Reduced Engine Mark 3 Supersonic speed to 200 units/s for smoother high-speed flight.
  • Added multiple rocket, camera, landing, and Syspo recovery fixes to keep moving celestial bodies and their surface interactions stable.

Version 1.0A

Day 10: major expansion, audio overhaul, ship upgrades, and the Syspo planetary system.

  • Added Syspo, a massive gas giant with a three-layer atmosphere and extreme-gravity core hazard.
  • Added two Syspo moons: Aurora, a lush habitable world, and Mileria, a rocky world rich in Titanium.
  • Added Titanium Ore / Ingots and Rainbow Opal as new resources.
  • Added Engine Mark 3 with Supersonic flight, 200 units/s speed, 300% fuel capacity, and 1% fuel use per second.
  • Added Warp Drive Mark 2 with Rainbow Opal fuel, 50/50 fuel consumption, and faster warp travel.
  • Added the Ship Inventory for removing installed rocket upgrades and returning them to the player's inventory.
  • Added the full Sound Settings mixer with Master, Interaction, Player, Nature, World & Special Effects, and Music controls.
  • Added the music system with 30-second minimum playback, smooth fades, planetary tracks, and exact-position stall music resume.
  • Added new Space Navigation Map destinations for Syspo, Aurora, and Mileria.
  • Added performance optimizations for clouds, rain, interaction checks, rendering, and per-frame calculations.
  • Fixed Moon/Cordelia liftoff, rocket HUD visibility, journal selling, Aurora lake rendering, and multiple rocket-flight/menu regressions.

  • Added the Space Navigation Map for travelling between celestial bodies.

  • Added dedicated takeoff/escape handling for Moon and Cordelia rockets, including the Moon liftoff fix.
